# National & International Reading Level Guides

The Reading & Literacy Hall includes multiple reading-level systems because families may encounter different terminology across schools, publishers, countries, libraries, or multilingual programs.

## Important Rule

**Do not treat these systems as exact conversions.**

They measure or describe different things.

---

## United States · Lexile

Common symbols:

- `BR` — Beginning Reader
- `L` — Lexile

The Hall includes K–8 spring national norm ranges.

These are presented as **norms**, not state proficiency standards.

A Lexile measure is not a complete reading profile. It does not replace direct observation of:

- decoding;
- fluency;
- vocabulary;
- comprehension;
- motivation;
- background knowledge;
- writing;
- disciplinary literacy.

---

## England

Common stage symbols:

- EYFS — Early Years Foundation Stage
- KS1 — Key Stage 1
- KS2 — Key Stage 2
- KS3 — Key Stage 3

Broad progression:

- early oral language and literacy;
- phonics and word reading;
- comprehension;
- increasingly independent reading;
- literature and nonfiction;
- increasingly challenging texts;
- analysis and discussion.

---

## Australia

The Hall surfaces several Australian literacy progression abbreviations:

- `PhA` — Phonological Awareness
- `PKW` — Phonic Knowledge & Word Recognition
- `FlY` — Fluency
- `UnT` — Understanding Texts

These progression markers should not be simplified into one grade-equivalent score.

---

## New Zealand

The Hall references early structured-literacy and reading phases such as:

- Kākano
- Tupu
- Māhuri
- Rākau

It also references Ready to Read colour progression language where applicable.

The purpose is orientation for families who encounter New Zealand resources—not forced conversion into U.S. grade labels.

---

## CEFR

The Common European Framework of Reference for Languages uses:

- Pre-A1
- A1
- A2
- B1
- B2
- C1
- C2

CEFR is most useful for **additional-language / multilingual proficiency**.

It should not be treated as a native-language school reading-grade equivalency chart.

---

## Commercial / Local Systems

Families may also encounter systems such as:

- GRL
- DRA
- ATOS
- publisher book bands
- local benchmark labels
- school-specific intervention levels

The Hall intentionally does not fabricate universal conversions among them.

Use the issuing system's current documentation.

# Khaemenes Seven Literacy Symbols

| Symbol | Skill Family |
|---|---|
| 🌱 | Emergent Language |
| 👂 | Sound Awareness |
| 🔤 | Code & Decoding |
| 📖 | Fluent Meaning |
| 🧩 | Word Knowledge |
| 🔎 | Evidence & Analysis |
| ✍ | Response & Communication |

These symbols indicate instructional domains.

They are **not prestige levels**.

A learner may be working at different levels across different literacy strands.
