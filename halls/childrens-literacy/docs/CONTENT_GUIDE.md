# Content Guide

## Purpose

This document describes how new books, literacy tools, modules, and resources should be added to the Hall.

---

## Adding Literature

A literature dossier should provide enough metadata for filtering and contextual teaching.

Recommended fields include:

- unique ID;
- title;
- author;
- region / tradition;
- era;
- age band;
- literary form;
- copyright / discovery status;
- category;
- short context;
- tags;
- study focus;
- discussion questions;
- lawful reading/discovery links.

---

## Copyright Rules

Use:

- public-domain texts;
- licensed resources;
- official library discovery pages;
- lawful read-aloud sources;
- bibliographic references;
- discussion and contextual material.

Do not add:

- unauthorized full copyrighted books;
- pirated PDFs;
- copied modern book text;
- unsafe mirror sites.

---

## Sensitive Historic Works

When a historic text contains harmful or outdated content:

1. preserve historical context;
2. identify the concern;
3. avoid presenting stereotypes as truth;
4. support age-appropriate discussion;
5. consider a wider-shelf companion;
6. include voices from affected communities where appropriate.

---

## Adding Literacy Tools

New tools should:

- serve a defined literacy strand;
- identify the intended developmental band;
- avoid pretending to diagnose;
- avoid random grading;
- preserve teacher/family authority;
- avoid generating an incoherent phonics sequence;
- connect reading to meaning.

---

## Adding Reading-Level Systems

Do not add a conversion chart unless the issuing organization itself supports the conversion.

A new level system should document:

- who created it;
- what it measures;
- the symbols it uses;
- whether it is national, state, commercial, or local;
- whether it is intended for native-language literacy or additional-language proficiency;
- the official/reference source.

---

## Source Quality

Prefer:

1. official national/state education sources;
2. national libraries;
3. established literacy research organizations;
4. major professional associations;
5. lawful library and public-domain collections.

Avoid relying on unsourced reading-level conversion blogs.
