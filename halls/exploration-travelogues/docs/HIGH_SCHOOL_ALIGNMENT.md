# High School Alignment

## Travelogues 201–202 · Grades 9–10
Students analyze:
- date and origin;
- author purpose and point of view;
- primary and secondary accounts;
- maps, data and text;
- evidence supporting claims;
- colonial/scientific records;
- environmental observation;
- counter-sources.

Reference:
https://www.thecorestandards.org/ELA-Literacy/RH/9-10/

## Travelogues 203 · Grades 11–12
Students evaluate:
- competing explanations;
- claims, reasoning and evidence;
- source uncertainty;
- translation/editorial mediation;
- forced movement and trauma-aware source use;
- ethical tourism;
- sustained research and argument.

Reference:
https://www.thecorestandards.org/ELA-Literacy/RH/11-12/
