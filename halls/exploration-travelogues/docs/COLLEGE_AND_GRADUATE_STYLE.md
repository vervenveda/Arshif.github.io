# College & Graduate-Style Progression

There is no single universal university curriculum for travel writing, mobility studies, historical geography or spatial humanities.

The 401–603 ladder therefore progresses through methods and competencies rather than claiming automatic equivalence to a specific institution.

## 401–403 · Undergraduate
- travel writing and mobility studies;
- primary-source analysis;
- historical geography;
- cartography;
- field observation;
- spatial humanities;
- ethical dossier creation;
- research writing.

## 501–503 · Advanced Undergraduate
- mobility/tourism/displacement ethics;
- global network geographies;
- advanced comparative method;
- environmental travel writing;
- advanced field methods;
- public humanities.

## 601–603 · Graduate-Style Independent Study
- historiography of exploration/empire;
- archive genealogy;
- digital spatial humanities;
- GIS/data planning;
- metadata and provenance;
- methods documentation;
- thesis/digital atlas/capstone;
- oral defense.
