# Content Guide

## Adding a Dossier
Include:
- unique ID;
- title;
- author/keeper/source tradition;
- civilization/context;
- era;
- genre/type;
- route/region;
- date;
- language/translation note;
- tags;
- concise summary;
- context;
- themes;
- study questions;
- lawful reading/map links.

## Ethical Framing
Do not equate all movement with exploration.

Differentiate:
- pilgrimage;
- trade;
- diplomacy;
- expedition;
- scientific travel;
- tourism;
- migration;
- diaspora;
- captivity;
- slavery;
- deportation;
- exile;
- displacement.

Do not invent missing Indigenous/local testimony to 'balance' a colonial account. Locate actual community, archival, oral-history or scholarly counter-sources.

## Copyright
Use public-domain, licensed or library/discovery links for modern copyrighted works rather than reproducing protected texts.
