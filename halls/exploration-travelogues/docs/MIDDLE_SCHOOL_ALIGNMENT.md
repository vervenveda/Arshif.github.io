# Middle School Alignment

## Travelogues 101 · Grade 6
Uses:
- primary/secondary source evidence;
- accurate source summary;
- author point of view;
- maps/visual information;
- fact, opinion and reasoned judgment;
- local route observation;
- map reading and spatial representation.

## Travelogues 102 · Grade 7
Adds:
- comparison of written/oral/mapped route knowledge;
- pilgrimage/diaspora context;
- perspective and access;
- specific cultural context;
- research and evidence-based discussion.

## Travelogues 103 · Grade 8
Adds:
- network thinking;
- maritime geography;
- source reliability;
- map/text integration;
- strongest evidence;
- increasingly independent research.
