Eighteen Formal Pathways

Study grows deeper without making knowledge “more available.”

Higher course numbers increase analytical, research, governance, archival, and capstone rigor. They never override community authority or convert restricted knowledge into academic content.

Storywork Studio

Eleven tools for responsible learning and documentation.

Research & Data Governance Studio

Advanced work begins with authority and responsibility.

CARE, OCAP®, Local Contexts, AIATSIS, UNDRIP, and other frameworks are presented with scope notes so learners do not treat one regional governance system as universal.

A classroom exercise using public sources is not the same thing as conducting research with Indigenous people or community-governed knowledge. Real research may require institutional ethics review, tribal/Nation/community approval, informed consent, Indigenous leadership, data-governance agreements, and other jurisdiction-specific requirements.
