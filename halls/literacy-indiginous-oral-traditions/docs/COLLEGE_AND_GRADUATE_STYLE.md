# College & Graduate-Style Progression

There is no single national university curriculum for Indigenous oral traditions or Indigenous studies.

The 401–603 ladder therefore uses scholarly-method and research-governance progression rather than claiming universal college equivalence.

## 401–403 · Undergraduate
- close source analysis;
- oral history and historiography;
- language/translation/transcription;
- comparative method;
- public-source research;
- ethics and consent distinctions;
- archive/access governance;
- annotated research documentation;
- community-accountable methods.

## 501–503 · Advanced Undergraduate
- Indigenous knowledge systems;
- law/kinship/ecology;
- advanced archival critique;
- Indigenous data sovereignty;
- cultural rights;
- digital humanities;
- public humanities;
- curriculum evaluation;
- partnership and reciprocity.

## 601–603 · Graduate-Style
- advanced seminar framing;
- historiography and epistemology;
- research ethics and governance;
- data-management/access planning;
- community review and accountability;
- substantial critical/digital/pedagogical capstone;
- oral defense.

The 600-series is internal Khaemenes architecture and does not create graduate credit or permission to conduct research.
