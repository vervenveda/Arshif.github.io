# Middle School Alignment

The 101–103 ladder uses widely portable source-literacy and ELA/history practices while keeping Indigenous specificity and protocol at the center.

## IOT 101 · Grade 6
- cite evidence;
- distinguish what a source states from prior assumption;
- identify central ideas;
- listen and discuss with evidence;
- identify speaker/source/community;
- compare oral, visual, and written memory;
- learn that restricted/community-governed knowledge is not a classroom entitlement.

## IOT 102 · Grade 7
- compare forms/media;
- analyze point of view;
- distinguish oral history from generic folklore labels;
- connect story, performance, place, and historical context;
- compare sources without erasing community difference.

## IOT 103 · Grade 8
- use strongest relevant evidence;
- compare multiple public sources;
- analyze relationships among story, land, law, ecology and seasonal knowledge;
- conduct responsible research and presentation;
- explain why protocol can limit reuse.

Reference standards and sources appear in `SOURCES.md`.
