# Content Guide

## Adding a Dossier
A dossier should identify:
- specific people / Nation / community;
- place / region;
- language where known;
- type of tradition or public-learning method;
- era / living status;
- concise summary;
- historical/cultural context;
- public study prompt;
- themes/tags;
- exact source links.

## Never Treat “Indigenous” as One Culture
Do not merge unrelated nations, regions, ceremonies, stories, symbols or worldviews into a pan-Indigenous composite.

## Restricted Knowledge
Do not add restricted, ceremonial, clan-owned, gender-specific, seasonal, initiated, private or otherwise governed knowledge merely because it appears somewhere online.

## Community-Authored Sources
Prefer:
1. Indigenous/Nation/community-authored sources;
2. tribal/Nation cultural institutions;
3. community-partnered educational resources;
4. responsible archives/museums with provenance and community relationships;
5. careful scholarship used transparently.

## AI
AI may assist with organization, comparison questions, source-checking structures, and writing frames.

AI must not be treated as:
- a knowledge keeper;
- a cultural authority;
- proof of permission;
- a source for restricted stories;
- a replacement for community review.
