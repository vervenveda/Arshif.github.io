# High School Alignment

## IOT 201–202 · Grades 9–10
The sequence emphasizes:
- source date and origin;
- primary/secondary/community-source distinctions;
- central ideas;
- point of view and mediation;
- reasoning and evidence;
- comparison across sources;
- research and presentation;
- written analysis;
- exact people/Nation/community/place/language naming.

## IOT 203 · Grades 11–12
The sequence adds:
- sustained research;
- source synthesis;
- ambiguity and conflicting accounts;
- advanced historical/contextual interpretation;
- law, kinship and knowledge-system analysis;
- formal argument and presentation;
- explicit methodological and ethical limits.

Common Core is used only as a recognizable U.S. skills benchmark. Formal state graduation/course-credit requirements remain jurisdiction-specific.
