# Digital Preservation & TEI

## Library of Congress preservation concepts
The Hall introduces:
- digital ingest;
- file-format sustainability;
- metadata;
- storage monitoring;
- preservation documentation;
- long-term access.

## TEI
TEI P5 provides guidelines for representing textual resources in structured electronic form.

The Hall treats TEI as an advanced study pathway, not as a claim that a simple browser exercise equals a professional critical edition.

## Suggested student digital-edition layers
- source/facsimile reference;
- diplomatic transcription;
- normalized reading text if justified;
- variants;
- editorial notes;
- metadata;
- rights/access;
- revision history;
- export/documentation.
