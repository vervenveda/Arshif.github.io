# High School Alignment

## Vault 201–202 · Grades 9–10
Students reconstruct evidence, compare textual witnesses, track translations/versions, use precise terminology and write source-grounded arguments.

## Vault 203 / 301–303 · Grades 11–12 / College Bridge
Students synthesize sources, analyze provenance/cultural heritage, research censorship cases, study textual criticism, and design preservation/digital-editing projects.

Common Core Writing 9–10 and 11–12 provide transferable argument/research benchmarks.
