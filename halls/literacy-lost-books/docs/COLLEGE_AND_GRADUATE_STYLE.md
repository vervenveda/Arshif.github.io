# College & Graduate-Style Progression

There is no single universal university curriculum for book history, archives, textual criticism or digital scholarly editing.

The 401–603 ladder progresses through:
- material-text analysis;
- book/manuscript history;
- censorship/public-memory studies;
- archival ethics and provenance;
- textual criticism and editorial theory;
- metadata and preservation;
- TEI/XML structured text;
- digital scholarly editions;
- historiography;
- critical editions / digital vaults / recovery studies.

The 600-series is graduate-style independent study, not institutional graduate credit.
