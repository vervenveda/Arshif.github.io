# Middle School Alignment

Vault 101–103 uses source literacy rather than sensational stories about “forbidden knowledge.”

## 101
Evidence, primary/secondary sources, summary, uncertainty and responsible dossier building.

## 102
Material texts, manuscripts, variants, source comparison and domain vocabulary.

## 103
Canon, challenge/ban terminology, public-memory institutions and evidence-based argument.

Common Core RH 6–8 and WHST 6–8 are used as recognizable U.S. source/writing benchmarks, not as a claim that every jurisdiction uses identical requirements.
