# Vault Studies 101–603 Course Catalog

## Vault 101 · What Survives? Lost Works & Evidence

**Level:** Middle School · Grade 6

**Modules:** What Does It Mean for a Work to Be Lost?; Evidence, Provenance & Degrees of Certainty; Fragment, Epitome, Quotation & Testimonium; Canon, Collection & Public Memory

**Outcomes**
- Distinguish lost, fragmented, banned, challenged and inaccessible.
- Use evidence to say what is known, inferred or uncertain.
- Explain how a quotation or summary can preserve part of a lost work.
- Build a respectful starter dossier with sources.

**Evidence:** Evidence chart · source card · mini dossier · discussion

**Alignment / boundary:** Grade 6–8 primary/secondary-source evidence, accurate summary and point of view.

## Vault 102 · Fragments, Manuscripts & the Life of a Text

**Level:** Middle School · Grade 7

**Modules:** Papyrus, Parchment & Palimpsest; Codicology · The Book as Object; Paleography · Reading Historical Hands; Manuscript Transmission & Scribal Variation

**Outcomes**
- Recognize manuscripts as physical historical evidence.
- Explain how copying creates variants.
- Practice basic codicology/paleography concepts without claiming expert authentication.
- Compare multiple witnesses or descriptions of the same work.

**Evidence:** Manuscript observation · variant exercise · vocabulary notebook · comparison

**Alignment / boundary:** Grade 7 source integration, structure, comparison and domain vocabulary.

## Vault 103 · Canon, Censorship & Public Memory

**Level:** Middle School · Grade 8

**Modules:** Canon, Collection & Public Memory; Apocrypha, Canon Formation & Border Texts; Challenge, Restriction, Removal & Ban; Intellectual Freedom, Libraries & Collection Policy; Banned & Challenged Literature Case Study; School Curricula, Age, Context & Contested Reading

**Outcomes**
- Explain that canon formation and censorship are different processes.
- Use challenge/ban terminology precisely.
- Compare how institutions preserve, restrict, remove or teach works.
- Write an evidence-based argument about access and context.

**Evidence:** Terminology audit · policy comparison · challenged-book case · argument

**Alignment / boundary:** Grade 8 evidence, source comparison, argument and research.

## Vault 201 · Lost Antiquity & Fragmentary Reconstruction

**Level:** High School · Grades 9–10

**Modules:** Fragment, Epitome, Quotation & Testimonium; Lost Literature of Antiquity; Papyrus, Parchment & Palimpsest; Manuscript Transmission & Scribal Variation; Introduction to Textual Criticism

**Outcomes**
- Reconstruct a lost-work dossier from fragments and testimonia.
- Distinguish primary witnesses from later summaries and scholarship.
- Identify uncertainty rather than filling gaps imaginatively.
- Write a defensible reconstruction argument.

**Evidence:** Fragment dossier · witness map · reconstruction essay · seminar

**Alignment / boundary:** Grades 9–10 argument, evidence, source evaluation and formal explanatory writing.

## Vault 202 · Scripture, Apocrypha & Canon Formation

**Level:** High School · Grades 9–10

**Modules:** Translation, Version & Redaction; Apocrypha, Canon Formation & Border Texts; The Book of Enoch as a Transmission Case; Early Christian Proto-texts & Fragmentary Gospels

**Outcomes**
- Study apocrypha and canon formation historically and comparatively.
- Track manuscript, language, translation and redaction differences.
- Avoid calling a text 'lost' when substantial witnesses survive.
- Separate theological claims from historical/textual evidence.

**Evidence:** Transmission chart · comparative source notes · analytical essay · discussion

**Alignment / boundary:** Grades 9–10 source-based argument, comparison, vocabulary and research.

## Vault 203 · Conquest, Destruction & Cultural Recovery

**Level:** High School · Grades 11–12

**Modules:** Conquest, Destruction & the Mexican Codices; War, Fire, Disaster & Documentary Loss; Colonial Archives & Counter-Archives; Provenance, Ownership, Repatriation & Ethical Stewardship; Conservation & Material Preservation

**Outcomes**
- Analyze documentary loss caused by conquest, conflict, disaster and archival power.
- Study the Mexican codices as a cultural-heritage case without treating surviving knowledge as ownerless.
- Evaluate provenance and stewardship questions.
- Synthesize multiple source types into sustained historical analysis.

**Evidence:** Case study · provenance memo · counter-archive · 6–8 page paper

**Alignment / boundary:** Grades 11–12 research, source synthesis, competing claims and formal argument.

## Vault 301 · Banned, Challenged & Restricted Books

**Level:** Advanced High School · College Bridge

**Modules:** Challenge, Restriction, Removal & Ban; Intellectual Freedom, Libraries & Collection Policy; Banned & Challenged Literature Case Study; School Curricula, Age, Context & Contested Reading; Copyright, Public Domain & Lawful Access

**Outcomes**
- Use ALA challenge/ban terminology accurately.
- Analyze documented cases without claiming every controversy is a ban.
- Compare access, age/context, curriculum, collection policy and rights questions.
- Develop a source-grounded public-information dossier.

**Evidence:** Challenge record · policy analysis · public explainer · oral defense

**Alignment / boundary:** Advanced civic/literary inquiry and evidence-based argument; not legal advice.

## Vault 302 · Textual Criticism, Translation & Scholarly Editing

**Level:** Advanced High School · College Bridge

**Modules:** Paleography · Reading Historical Hands; Manuscript Transmission & Scribal Variation; Introduction to Textual Criticism; Translation, Version & Redaction; TEI / XML & Textual Encoding

**Outcomes**
- Reconstruct arguments from multiple textual witnesses.
- Explain editorial choices and uncertainty.
- Track translation and version differences.
- Encode a small public-domain sample using structured markup concepts.

**Evidence:** Variant table · editorial rationale · translation note · introductory TEI exercise

**Alignment / boundary:** College-bridge textual scholarship and digital humanities.

## Vault 303 · Archives, Preservation & Digital Memory

**Level:** Advanced High School · College Bridge

**Modules:** Provenance, Ownership, Repatriation & Ethical Stewardship; Conservation & Material Preservation; Digitization, Imaging & OCR; Metadata, Cataloging & Discovery; Sustainable Digital Preservation

**Outcomes**
- Explain preservation as continuing stewardship rather than one-time digitization.
- Design metadata and access notes for a small archive.
- Compare material and digital preservation risks.
- Create a rights-aware digital-vault prototype.

**Evidence:** Preservation plan · metadata set · risk audit · prototype

**Alignment / boundary:** College-bridge archival and information-literacy pathway.

## Vault 401 · Book History, Manuscript Studies & Material Texts

**Level:** College · Undergraduate Foundation

**Modules:** Papyrus, Parchment & Palimpsest; Codicology · The Book as Object; Paleography · Reading Historical Hands; Manuscript Transmission & Scribal Variation; Provenance, Ownership, Repatriation & Ethical Stewardship; Conservation & Material Preservation

**Outcomes**
- Analyze books/manuscripts as material objects with production and ownership histories.
- Use codicological and paleographic evidence cautiously.

**Evidence:** Object description · provenance chain · seminar · 8–10 page paper

**Alignment / boundary:** Undergraduate book-history / manuscript-studies foundation.

## Vault 402 · Censorship, Libraries & the Politics of Public Memory

**Level:** College · Undergraduate Foundation

**Modules:** Canon, Collection & Public Memory; Challenge, Restriction, Removal & Ban; Intellectual Freedom, Libraries & Collection Policy; Banned & Challenged Literature Case Study; School Curricula, Age, Context & Contested Reading; Copyright, Public Domain & Lawful Access

**Outcomes**
- Analyze censorship and access through documented institutional processes.
- Distinguish selection, weeding, challenge, restriction and ban.
- Study canon/public memory without reducing disagreement to one political direction.

**Evidence:** Policy dossier · historical case comparison · seminar · research paper

**Alignment / boundary:** Undergraduate library/history/public-humanities pathway.

## Vault 603 · The Vault Thesis · Critical Edition, Recovery Study or Digital Archive

**Level:** Graduate-Style · Thesis / Capstone

**Modules:** Historiography of Loss, Recovery & Reception; Critical Edition / Recovery Project Capstone; Thesis / Digital Vault · Memory, Loss & Access

**Outcomes**
- Define an original bounded contribution using lawful and ethically available evidence.
- Build a transparent source/witness corpus.

**Alignment / boundary:** Highest Khaemenes Vault pathway; graduate-style independent work, not an accredited thesis.
