# Content Guide

## Adding a Vault Dossier
Record:
- title;
- creator / attributed creator;
- date/period;
- cultural/language context;
- survival status;
- witness types;
- provenance;
- rights/access status;
- concise evidence-based note;
- what remains uncertain;
- lawful sources.

## Required status discipline
Do not label a work “lost” merely because it is:
- out of print;
- difficult to access;
- untranslated;
- challenged;
- removed from one curriculum;
- copyrighted;
- preserved in a different canon/community.

## Modern copyrighted books
Link to libraries, publishers, authorized excerpts, databases or discovery tools—not questionable full-text mirrors.
