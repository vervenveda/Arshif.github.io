# Archive, Access & Recovery Ethics

## Do not turn absence into certainty
A responsible Vault clearly separates:
- surviving evidence;
- later testimony;
- strong inference;
- disputed inference;
- speculation.

## Cultural heritage
A digitized or physically displaced object is not automatically ownerless or culturally unrestricted.

## Censorship terminology
A challenge is not automatically a ban. Document the institution, date, action, process and outcome.

## Copyright
Preservation and research do not erase copyright. Use lawful discovery, library, licensed and public-domain pathways.

## Recovery
Do not invent lost passages or missing community testimony to make a narrative feel complete.

## AI
AI can help structure research notes and compare supplied evidence. It cannot authenticate a manuscript, reconstruct an unknown original with certainty, determine ownership, or grant cultural permission.
