# Competency Framework

## 1. ◌ Loss, Survival & Degrees of Certainty

Distinguish destroyed, missing, fragmentary, inaccessible, suppressed, disputed and surviving works; state uncertainty clearly.

## 2. § Provenance, Witnesses & Source Criticism

Trace where evidence comes from, who transmitted it, and what each witness can actually support.

## 3. ⌘ Manuscripts, Codicology & Paleography

Read the book as a material object and use script/material evidence cautiously.

## 4. ≋ Textual Criticism, Variants & Editorial Method

Compare witnesses, variants, omissions, additions and editorial choices without inventing a lost original.

## 5. 文 Translation, Redaction & Version History

Track language, translation, adaptation and editorial mediation across versions.

## 6. ◇ Canon, Reception & Public Memory

Analyze how communities and institutions preserve, privilege, marginalize or reinterpret texts.

## 7. ⚖ Censorship, Challenge & Intellectual Freedom

Use precise challenge/restriction/ban terminology and evaluate policy/source evidence fairly.

## 8. ⌂ Cultural Heritage, Provenance & Stewardship

Study destruction, colonial archives, ownership/custody, community authority and ethical access.

## 9. ▦ Conservation, Digitization & Metadata

Design preservation, imaging, cataloging and discovery records with rights and provenance information.

## 10. </> Digital Text, TEI & Scholarly Editions

Represent textual structure, variants, metadata and editorial responsibility in digital form.

## 11. ✦ Research, Critical Edition & Capstone

Synthesize evidence, scholarship, methods, ethics and preservation into a substantial project.
