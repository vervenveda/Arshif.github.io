A quiet scholarly register for works that survive unevenly: fragments, quotations, translations, manuscripts, disputed canons, destroyed documentary heritage, challenged books, and digital recoveries.

This Vault does not erase anyone’s canon — it simply remembers. Its task is not to turn absence into certainty, but to preserve evidence, distinguish kinds of loss, and keep responsible pathways to memory open.

SOURCE HYGIENE
