# Middle School Alignment

Theatre 101–103 is organized around the National Core Arts Standards theatre processes:

- Creating
- Performing / Presenting / Producing
- Responding
- Connecting

The sequence moves from ensemble/story foundations into world-theatre forms and finally script-to-stage analysis, original creation, design and rights awareness.

The goal is not early specialization. It is broad theatre literacy, collaboration, reflection and safe creative practice.
