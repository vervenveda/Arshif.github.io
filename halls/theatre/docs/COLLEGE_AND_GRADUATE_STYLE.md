# College & Graduate-Style Progression

The National Association of Schools of Theatre maintains current standards and guidelines for accredited theatre institutions and degree/non-degree programs.

Official reference:
https://nast.arts-accredit.org/accreditation/standards-guidelines/handbook/

Khaemenes Theatre 401–603 is informed by the breadth of higher-education theatre practice but is not NAST accreditation.

## 401–403 · Undergraduate Foundation
- theatre history / dramatic literature;
- acting / directing;
- dramaturgy;
- design;
- stage management;
- production.

## 501–503 · Advanced Undergraduate
- comparative/global performance;
- new work / devising;
- theatre leadership / production ethics;
- sustained research and portfolio practice.

## 601–603 · Graduate-Style Independent Study
- historiography and theory;
- practice-as-research;
- advanced performance/design/directing inquiry;
- thesis / production / script / research capstone;
- oral defense.

Institutional degree requirements vary.
