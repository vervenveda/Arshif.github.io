# Rehearsal, Rights & Safety

## Script / Performance Rights

Reading availability and performance permission are not the same thing.

Before public performance, adaptation, recording or streaming, verify:
- copyright status;
- translation/edition rights;
- performance license;
- adaptation permission;
- recording/streaming permission;
- venue/territory restrictions.

## Consent

Use clear rehearsal boundaries around:
- touch;
- personal disclosure;
- improvisation;
- recording;
- photography;
- costume/body concerns;
- religious/cultural material;
- accessibility needs.

## Specialized Physical Work

This Hall does **not** teach or certify hazardous theatrical technique.

Combat, weapons, falls, lifts, aerial work, rigging, pyrotechnics, stage effects, intimacy choreography and other specialized physical work require appropriately trained supervision and venue-specific procedures.

## Cultural Practice

Do not treat living, sacred or community-governed performance traditions as generic aesthetic material. Research context, authority, adaptation ethics and permissions.
