# High School Alignment

The National Core Arts Standards recognize three high-school achievement bands rather than assigning all theatre achievement strictly by grade:

- Proficient
- Accomplished
- Advanced

Theatre 201–203 roughly moves through those bands, while Theatre 301–303 functions as an advanced/college-bridge layer.

Evidence includes:
- script analysis;
- improvisation;
- acting;
- design concept;
- dramaturgy;
- directing;
- original writing/devising;
- feedback/revision;
- reflection;
- performance/context research.
