# Content Guide

## Adding a Play Card

Record:
- unique ID;
- title;
- creator / tradition;
- era;
- culture;
- genre/form;
- approximate date;
- original language;
- rights status;
- tags;
- concise context;
- themes;
- lawful reading/discovery/licensing links;
- study/rehearsal activity.

## Rights Status

Use:
- `public-domain` only as a reading trail indicator;
- `rights-check` whenever performance/translation/adaptation status is not clearly free.

A public-domain original can still have copyrighted translations, adaptations, editions, recordings and designs.

## Living Traditions

Use precise cultural context and avoid generic imitation of sacred or community-governed performance.
