# International Advanced Alignment

## IB Diploma Programme Theatre

Current IB Theatre emphasizes theatre-making as:
- creator;
- designer;
- director;
- performer;
- individual artist;
- ensemble collaborator.

It emphasizes a creative process of:
- inquiring;
- developing;
- presenting;
- evaluating.

Official reference:
https://www.ibo.org/university-admission/latest-curriculum-updates/theatre/

## Cambridge International AS & A Level Drama 9482

Current Cambridge Drama develops:
- performing;
- devising;
- researching;
- analysis;
- creation;
- interpretation;
- practical work from performance texts;
- devised material;
- individual and group practice;
- reflective theatrical practice.

Official reference:
https://www.cambridgeinternational.org/programmes-and-qualifications/view/cambridge-international-as-and-a-level-drama-9482/

Theatre 301–303 uses these practices as references but does not claim program authorization or examination equivalence.
