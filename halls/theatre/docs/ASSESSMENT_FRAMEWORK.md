# Assessment Framework

Evidence may include:
- ensemble/devising work;
- monologue and scene studies;
- script analysis;
- performance reflection;
- dramaturgy packets;
- director concepts;
- design presentations;
- stage-management documents;
- original plays;
- devised performances;
- production portfolios;
- research essays;
- oral defenses.

Competencies are recorded as:
- Not observed
- Developing
- Secure

A strong actor may still be developing research or production literacy. A strong designer may still be developing ensemble performance. Keep those differences visible.
