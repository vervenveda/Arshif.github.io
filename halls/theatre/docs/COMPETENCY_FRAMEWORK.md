# Theatre Competency Framework

## 1. ✦ Creating & Devising

Generate, develop, revise and complete original theatrical ideas individually and collaboratively.

## 2. 🎭 Acting, Character & Ensemble

Use given circumstances, objectives, tactics, listening, relationship, status, timing and ensemble awareness.

## 3. ◉ Voice, Movement & Presence

Use breath, clarity, physical choice, rhythm, space and style while respecting safe personal and ensemble boundaries.

## 4. § Script Analysis & Dramatic Literature

Analyze action, structure, language, character, genre, stage directions, themes and performance possibilities.

## 5. ⌖ Theatre History & Performance Context

Connect work to historical, cultural, social, political, technological and audience contexts.

## 6. ◎ Directing & Dramaturgy

Build concept, research, rehearsal questions, staging logic, actor support and audience relationship.

## 7. ◇ Design & Visual/Aural Storytelling

Integrate scenic, costume, lighting, sound, props, make-up/graphics and practical production needs.

## 8. ▦ Stage Management & Production Literacy

Use schedules, reports, tracking, cueing, communication, budgeting awareness, collaboration and production documentation.

## 9. ⚖ Rights, Ethics, Consent & Safety

Distinguish reading/performance/adaptation rights; use consent, representation, accessibility and appropriate trained supervision.

## 10. ¶ Research, Response & Critical Reflection

Use sources, production evidence, critique, feedback, interpretation and reflective evaluation.

## 11. ★ Portfolio, Leadership & Capstone

Synthesize performance, writing, research, production and reflection into a substantial theatrical contribution.

The Hall intentionally does not collapse all theatre learning into one score.
