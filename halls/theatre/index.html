Eighteen Theatre Pathways

From first ensemble work to thesis-level theatre practice.

101–103 build story, ensemble, world-theatre literacy and script-to-stage practice. 201–303 move through high-school Proficient, Accomplished and Advanced work. 401–603 progress into theatre history, performance, directing, design, dramaturgy, production, new work, research and capstone practice.

Course-numbering boundary: Theatre 101–603 are internal Khaemenes course codes. They do not automatically confer IB, Cambridge, college-transfer, graduate, professional, accreditation or transcript credit.
REHEARSAL SAFETY

Artistic risk is not the same thing as physical risk.

The Hall may help plan safe discussion, ordinary rehearsal structure and low-risk exercises. Combat, weapons, falls, lifts, rigging, pyrotechnics, intimacy choreography and other specialized physical work require appropriate trained supervision and venue-specific safety practice.
