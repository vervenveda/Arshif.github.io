Bible & History Parallels Hall · 101–603

A faith-aware and evidence-disciplined study of Scripture alongside inscriptions, archaeology, archives, manuscripts, ancient writers, geography, chronology, and scholarly debate—from middle school foundations through graduate-style independent research.

Study posture: Each inquiry keeps four lenses visible: (1) what the text says, (2) what external evidence directly attests, (3) what remains debated or uncertain, and (4) devotional / theological reflection. The fourth lens is meaningful within Khaemenes faith study, but it is not silently presented as an archaeological or historical finding.
