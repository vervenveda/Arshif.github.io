# Middle School Alignment

## 101 · Source Literacy
Students distinguish textual claims, external sources, inference, debate and faith reflection.

## 102 · Egypt / Early Israel
Students work with chronology, maps, the Merneptah Stele and archaeological models through careful language.

## 103 · Israel / Judah / Neighbors
Students compare Mesha, Assyrian, Lachish and Siloam evidence with biblical texts.

### Religious-Literacy Boundary
The AAR K–12 guidelines distinguish devotional religious formation from non-devotional academic religious studies appropriate to public-school settings.

Khaemenes retains devotional prayer/application in its original course while labeling those parts separately from historical evidence.
