# Evidence & Historical Method

## Four-Lens Method

Always separate:
1. text;
2. external evidence;
3. debate / uncertainty;
4. faith / theological reflection.

## Evidence Language

Prefer:
- attests;
- mentions;
- dates;
- contextualizes;
- is consistent with;
- supports a limited conclusion;
- is commonly interpreted as;
- remains disputed.

Avoid:
- “proves the entire Bible”;
- “archaeology disproves faith”;
- “all scholars agree” when they do not;
- using absence of evidence mechanically;
- treating a museum caption as the artifact itself;
- treating unprovenanced antiquities as if they had secure archaeological context.

## Archaeology

Use:
- provenience;
- context;
- stratigraphy;
- assemblage;
- dating method;
- publication history;
- uncertainty.

## Ancient Texts

Record:
- author / institution;
- date;
- genre;
- audience;
- language;
- textual witness;
- translation;
- damage/restoration;
- rhetoric / political purpose.

## Devotional Reflection

Prayer and theological application are preserved as meaningful Khaemenes faith practices. They are labeled so the academic claim remains transparent.
