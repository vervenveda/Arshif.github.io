# Bible & History Parallels 101–603 Course Catalog

## Bible & History 101 · Scripture, Sources & Historical Evidence

**Level:** Middle School · Grade 6

**Modules:** Text · External Evidence · Debate · Faith Reflection; Primary, Secondary & Tertiary Sources; Chronology, Synchronisms & Historical Dating; Archaeology: Context, Stratigraphy & Limits; Epigraphy & Inscription Method

### Outcomes
- Separate what a biblical text says from what an external source directly attests.
- Recognize artifacts, inscriptions, manuscripts and later scholarship as different source types.
- Use careful terms such as supports, contextualizes, suggests, disputes, and remains unknown.
- Keep devotional reflection distinct from the historical-evidence claim.

**Evidence:** Claim/evidence chart · artifact card · timeline exercise · reflection

**Alignment / boundary:** Religious-literacy and historical-source foundations; appropriate devotional elements are clearly labeled as Khaemenes faith practice.

## Bible & History 102 · Egypt, Canaan & Early Israel

**Level:** Middle School · Grade 7

**Modules:** Egypt, Merneptah & Early Israel; Exodus, Settlement & Historical Method; Chronology, Synchronisms & Historical Dating; Archaeology: Context, Stratigraphy & Limits; Historical Geography & Biblical Worlds

### Outcomes
- Study the Merneptah Stele and early-Israel chronology with bounded conclusions.
- Compare biblical narratives with Egyptian and Levantine contexts without forcing agreement.
- Read maps, routes, settlement patterns and chronology as evidence.
- Describe major scholarly questions without turning uncertainty into disbelief or proof.

**Evidence:** Merneptah dossier · route map · evidence ladder · source comparison

**Alignment / boundary:** Middle-school history/source literacy plus faith-and-history distinction.

## Bible & History 103 · Israel, Judah & Neighbor Kingdoms

**Level:** Middle School · Grade 8

**Modules:** Moab, Israel & the Mesha Stele; Assyria, Kurkh & the Black Obelisk; Sennacherib, Lachish & Hezekiah; Jerusalem Water Systems & the Siloam Inscription; Epigraphy & Inscription Method

### Outcomes
- Compare Mesha, Assyrian, Lachish and Siloam evidence with biblical texts.
- Identify royal propaganda and other ancient genres.
- Use names, places, offices and events as contextual anchors without overclaiming.
- Write an evidence-based historical comparison.

**Evidence:** Mesha comparison · Assyria source map · Lachish analysis · short research essay

**Alignment / boundary:** High-school-readiness source comparison, argument and contextual history.

## Bible & History 201 · Exile, Babylon, Persia & Return

**Level:** High School · Grades 9–10

**Modules:** Babylonian Chronicles & Jerusalem 597 BCE; Jehoiachin Ration Tablets & Exile Daily Life; Persia, Cyrus & Restoration Policy; Ancient Archives & Administrative Texts; Historiography & Scholarly Disagreement

### Outcomes
- Use chronicles and administrative records to reconstruct imperial context.
- Compare exile/return narratives with Babylonian and Persian evidence.
- Analyze how genre and purpose affect what sources include or omit.
- Write a formal argument that states uncertainty and competing interpretations.

**Evidence:** Chronicle analysis · ration-tablet dossier · Cyrus comparison · 5–7 page paper

**Alignment / boundary:** High-school historical research, argument and evidence synthesis.

## Bible & History 202 · Second Temple Judaism & Textual Worlds

**Level:** High School · Grades 9–10

**Modules:** Second Temple Judaism & the Deuterocanon; Dead Sea Scrolls, Scribes & Manuscript Worlds; Hellenistic Judea, Hasmoneans & Cultural Change; Canon Formation & Reception History; Textual Criticism · Hebrew Bible & New Testament

### Outcomes
- Understand the Second Temple period as diverse Jewish history rather than a single background block.
- Label Deuterocanon/Apocrypha and Jewish/Christian collections accurately by tradition.
- Use Dead Sea Scrolls and manuscript evidence to discuss textual transmission.
- Explain how canon history and textual criticism differ.

**Evidence:** Tradition-labeling chart · DSS source study · manuscript comparison · seminar

**Alignment / boundary:** High-school religion/history literacy and advanced source interpretation.

## Bible & History 301 · Biblical Interpretation & Historical Method
