# High School Alignment

## 201
Exile, Babylon, Persia, return, chronicles and administrative records.

## 202
Second Temple Judaism, Deuterocanon, Dead Sea Scrolls, canon history and textual transmission.

## 203
Roman Judea, Pilate, Gallio, Erastus, ancient literary witnesses and New Testament context.

- archaeology;
- epigraphy;
- material culture;
- manuscripts;
- canon;
- textual criticism;
- formal research writing.
