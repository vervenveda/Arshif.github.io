# Archaeology, Provenance & Ethics

The Hall treats archaeology as an independent evidence discipline, not as a prop for a predetermined conclusion.

## Required Questions
- Was the object excavated?
- What is its provenience?
- Is the archaeological context secure?
- How was it dated?
- Where is it held?
- Is there a catalog / excavation publication?
- Is the inscription damaged or restored?
- Are readings disputed?
- Has authenticity been challenged?
- Are cultural-heritage or ownership issues relevant?

## Ethics
Current ASOR scholarship emphasizes ethical practice across classroom, field, museum and public archaeology, including cultural heritage and responsible professional conduct.
