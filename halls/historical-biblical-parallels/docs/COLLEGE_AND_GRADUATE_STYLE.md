# College & Graduate-Style Progression

There is no single universal university curriculum called "Bible & History Parallels."

The 401–603 sequence therefore progresses through recognizable fields and methods reflected in Society of Biblical Literature resources and publishing programs:

- Hebrew Bible / Ancient Near East
- New Testament / Early Christianity
- Early Judaism
- archaeology and biblical studies
- ancient texts
- textual criticism
- reception history
- research methods
- digital research
- advanced historiography

Official SBL resources:
https://www.sbl-site.org/resources/
https://www.sbl-site.org/sbl-press/browse-books/archaeology-and-biblical-studies/
https://www.sbl-site.org/sbl-press/browse-books/resource-for-biblical-study/

The Khaemenes 600-series is graduate-style independent study, not institutional graduate credit.
