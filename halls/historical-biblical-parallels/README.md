## Four-Lens Study Posture

Every advanced inquiry separates:

1. **What the text says**
2. **What external evidence directly attests**
3. **What remains debated / uncertain**
4. **Devotional or theological reflection**

Khaemenes may intentionally include prayer and faith reflection. Those are preserved and respected, but they are not silently presented as archaeological or historical findings.

## Academic / Religious-Literacy Boundary

The American Academy of Religion distinguishes devotional formation from non-devotional academic religious studies in public-school settings. Khaemenes is free to preserve its own faith practice, but the Hall keeps those categories visible so historical claims can still be evaluated on evidence.

SBL teaching resources explicitly support biblical studies in K–12 and college contexts and emphasize critical reading, writing, contextual interpretation and study of cultures/beliefs.
