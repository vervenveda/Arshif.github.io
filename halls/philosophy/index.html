Eighteen Course Pathways

One Philosophy Hall that grows with the learner.

101–103 build questions, concepts, evidence, dialogue, and world-philosophy foundations. 201–303 increase argument, counterargument, research, contemporary issues, and independent voice. 401–603 move into undergraduate depth, scholarly conversation, advanced method, and thesis-level independent work.

Course-numbering boundary: Khaemenes 101–603 codes describe internal rigor and sequencing. They do not automatically confer AP, IB, Cambridge, college-transfer, graduate, accreditation, or transcript credit.
