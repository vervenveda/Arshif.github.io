# Content Guide

## Adding a Module
Preserve:
- unique ID;
- title;
- era;
- branch;
- tradition;
- learner level;
- summary;
- essential questions;
- key ideas;
- core texts/traditions;
- study practice;
- lawful/reference links;
- contextual dossier.

## Adding a Thinker
Preserve:
- exact name;
- culture/tradition;
- dates;
- era;
- branch;
- civilization/context;
- concise philosophical contribution;
- public/reference reading pathways.

## Comparative Philosophy
Do not force all traditions into categories inherited from one philosophical culture.

Document:
- original-language terms where relevant;
- translation choices;
- genre differences;
- historical institutions;
- living/sacred/community context where relevant;
- genuine conceptual difference.

## Copyright
Do not reproduce modern copyrighted philosophical texts in full without permission.

Use public-domain texts, reference sources, licensed resources, or lawful library/discovery pathways.
