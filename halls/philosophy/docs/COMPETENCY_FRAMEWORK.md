# Philosophy Competency Framework

## 1. ? Questions & Conceptual Clarity

Formulate genuine philosophical questions; define terms; detect ambiguity, hidden assumptions, and conceptual shifts.

## 2. ⊢ Logic, Argument & Counterexample

Reconstruct premises/conclusions, test validity and evidence, distinguish deduction/induction, identify fallacies, and use counterexamples.

## 3. ¶ Close Reading & Interpretation

Read primary philosophical texts closely, summarize charitably, distinguish author claims from interpretation, and use exact textual evidence.

## 4. ◉ Knowledge, Evidence & Intellectual Humility

Evaluate justification, sources, testimony, perception, inference, uncertainty, and what a claim does not establish.

## 5. ⚖ Ethics & Practical Reasoning

Compare moral frameworks, duties, virtues, consequences, care, justice, responsibility, and applied cases.

## 6. ∞ Metaphysics, Mind & Science

Analyze being, identity, causation, time, self, consciousness, freedom, explanation, science, and technology.

## 7. ⌂ Political & Social Philosophy

Analyze authority, rights, liberty, justice, institutions, ideology, power, labor, race, gender, coloniality, and liberation.

## 8. 🌍 Comparative & World Philosophy

Compare traditions with attention to language, history, genre, translation, institutions, difference, and canon formation.

## 9. ◎ Dialogue, Seminar & Oral Defense

Listen, question, disagree charitably, respond to objections, lead discussion, and defend a position without treating debate as combat.

## 10. § Research, Citation & Scholarly Conversation

Develop research questions, distinguish primary/secondary sources, evaluate scholarship, cite, synthesize, and enter an existing debate.

## 11. ✦ Synthesis, Thesis & Public Practice

Integrate methods, texts, objections, research, and reflection into a substantial project with a clear philosophical contribution.

## Principle

The Hall does not calculate one philosophical-intelligence score.

A learner can be strong in ethics and still be developing logic; strong in close reading and still be developing oral defense; strong in comparative philosophy and still be developing research documentation.

Progress should remain visible by competency.
