# Middle School Alignment

Philosophy 101–103 uses argument, reading, writing, research, and discussion expectations that are appropriate to Grades 6–8 while keeping philosophical inquiry central.

## Philosophy 101 · Grade 6
- clear claims and reasons;
- relevant evidence;
- source credibility;
- factual summary distinct from opinion;
- questions, definitions, premises and conclusions;
- respectful dialogue.

## Philosophy 102 · Grade 7
- comparison of philosophical positions;
- source/context awareness;
- point of view;
- explanatory and argumentative writing;
- evidence-based discussion.

## Philosophy 103 · Grade 8
- strongest relevant evidence;
- comparison of accounts;
- conceptual precision;
- counterargument;
- short research;
- source evaluation and citation.
