# High School Alignment

## Philosophy 201–202 · Grades 9–10
Students increasingly:
- formulate precise claims;
- distinguish claims from counterclaims;
- use valid reasoning and relevant/sufficient evidence;
- examine complex concepts;
- conduct research;
- evaluate sources;
- revise for clarity and formal style.

Reference:
https://www.thecorestandards.org/ELA-Literacy/W/9-10/

## Philosophy 203 · Grades 11–12
Students increase:
- precision;
- depth;
- fair and thorough counterargument;
- synthesis;
- sustained research;
- evaluation of strengths/limits;
- formal philosophical writing and presentation.

Reference:
https://www.thecorestandards.org/ELA-Literacy/W/11-12/

Formal graduation/course-credit rules remain jurisdiction-specific.
