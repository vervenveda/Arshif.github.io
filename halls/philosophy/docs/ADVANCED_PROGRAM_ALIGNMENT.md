# Advanced Program Alignment

## IB Diploma Programme Philosophy
The current IB philosophy course emphasizes **doing philosophy**: systematic critical inquiry, exploration of complex philosophical concepts/questions, examination of one's own and others' perspectives, independent philosophical voice, philosophical themes, prescribed text, and philosophical analysis.

Official reference:
https://www.ibo.org/programmes/diploma-programme/curriculum/individuals-and-societies/philosophy/

Philosophy 301 is especially compatible with those practices.

## Cambridge International AS & A Level Thinking Skills 9694
Cambridge Thinking Skills develops critical thinking, reasoning, problem solving, evidence evaluation, reasoned decision-making, and evidence-based argument.

Official reference:
https://www.cambridgeinternational.org/programmes-and-qualifications/cambridge-international-as-and-a-level-thinking-skills-9694/

Philosophy 302–303 use those reasoning practices as a reference point.
