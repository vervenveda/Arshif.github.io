# College & Graduate-Style Progression

There is no single national university philosophy curriculum.

The 401–603 sequence is therefore organized around philosophical practices and the American Philosophical Association's descriptions of undergraduate philosophical development rather than pretending that one course numbering system is universal.

## Philosophy 401–403 · Undergraduate Foundations
- critical reading;
- clear writing and speaking;
- philosophical problems;
- interpretation of texts;
- balanced exposition;
- argument construction and appraisal;
- history and breadth;
- depth in major areas.

## Philosophy 501–503 · Advanced Undergraduate
- comparative/global philosophy;
- history of canon and translation;
- contemporary applied areas;
- research questions;
- scholarly source evaluation;
- seminar leadership;
- sustained research writing.

## Philosophy 601–603 · Graduate-Style Independent Study
- historiography;
- method;
- advanced bibliography;
- scholarly conversation;
- original argument;
- conference-style presentation;
- substantial seminar paper;
- thesis/capstone.
