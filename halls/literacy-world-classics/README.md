# Khaemenes Academy · ARSHIF World Classics & Humanities Hall

**All-ages archive · Formal curriculum spine from High School through University**

This repository expands the original **ARSHIF Hall of World Classics** into a complete world-literature and humanities resource hub.

The original archive is preserved with:

- **80 world classics**
- **34 civilizations / traditions**
- historical context
- themes
- author / language / date / manuscript information
- edition and translation guidance
- lawful external reading / discovery links
- draggable dossier windows
- browser-saved personal notes

The expanded Hall adds:

- 6 study pathways;
- 33 curriculum modules;
- 11 scholarly skill strands;
- 11 research and humanities tools;
- reading status and favorites;
- curriculum completion tracking;
- local portfolio export;
- mentor / NAIB-ready summary copy;
- high-school standards crosswalk;
- advanced high-school AP / IB / Cambridge reference alignment;
- university-oriented research and seminar progression.

## Core Principle

**The archive is open to all ages. The formal academic spine begins at high school and rises through advanced undergraduate work.**

“All ages” does not mean every work is appropriate for every reader. Families and educators should choose works according to readiness and context.

## Six Study Pathways

1. All Ages · Explorer
2. High School · Grades 9–10
3. High School · Grades 11–12
4. Advanced High School · AP / IB / Cambridge
5. University · 100–200 Level
6. University · 300–400 Level

The advanced pathways are **alignment / preparation frameworks**, not claims that this static Hall independently grants AP, IB, Cambridge, university, accreditation, or state credit.
