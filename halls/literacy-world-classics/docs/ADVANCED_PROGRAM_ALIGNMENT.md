# Advanced Program Alignment

This Hall can support advanced high-school study but does not independently grant external-program credit or credentials.

## AP English Literature and Composition

The Hall supports practice in:

- character;
- setting;
- plot and structure;
- narrator / speaker;
- figurative language;
- literary argumentation;
- short fiction;
- poetry;
- longer fiction and drama;
- works across periods.

Official reference:
https://apcentral.collegeboard.org/courses/ap-english-literature-and-composition

## AP English Language and Composition

The Hall’s philosophy, testimony, political thought, essays, contextual materials, and research tools can support:

- rhetorical situation;
- claims and evidence;
- reasoning and organization;
- style;
- synthesis;
- argument.

Official reference:
https://apcentral.collegeboard.org/courses/ap-english-language-and-composition

## International Baccalaureate · Language A

The Hall supports:

- literature across cultures, periods, forms, and places;
- context of production and reception;
- critical analysis;
- comparative reading;
- oral and written expression;
- texts in translation;
- sustained independent analysis.

Official reference:
https://www.ibo.org/programmes/diploma-programme/curriculum/language-and-literature/language-a-language-and-literature/

## Cambridge International AS & A Level Literature in English

The Hall supports:

- prose;
- poetry;
- drama;
- reading and analysis;
- wider reading;
- critical context;
- effective written communication.

Official reference:
https://www.cambridgeinternational.org/programmes-and-qualifications/cambridge-international-as-and-a-level-english-literature-9695/

## Program Integrity

Teachers using this Hall for an AP, IB, Cambridge, dual-enrollment, or other recognized program must still satisfy that program’s current official requirements, approved syllabus/course-audit rules, assessment rules, and school policies.
