# Content Guide

## Adding a Work

Preserve the dossier model:

- unique ID;
- title;
- ribbon / tradition label;
- civilization / cultural tradition;
- category;
- searchable tags;
- short metadata line;
- author;
- original language;
- date;
- manuscript / textual transmission information;
- historical context;
- major themes;
- edition / translation guidance;
- lawful external reading or discovery links.

## Sacred / Living Traditions

Treat living religious, Indigenous, oral, and sacred traditions with specificity and respect.

Avoid implying that:

- one text represents an entire culture;
- a translation is culturally neutral;
- sacred material is merely folklore;
- oral traditions are inferior to print traditions.

## Translation

Where possible:

- identify the original language;
- name a responsible edition or translator;
- encourage comparison of translations;
- distinguish translation choices from facts about the source text.

## Copyright

Do not add unauthorized full modern texts.

Use:

- public-domain sources;
- lawful library discovery;
- publisher/author resources;
- licensed databases;
- bibliographic links;
- excerpts only where legally appropriate.

## Academic Level

Do not label a work itself as “Grade 10” or “University” unless there is a defensible curricular reason.

The Hall primarily levels **the task and method**, not the inherent worth of the text.
