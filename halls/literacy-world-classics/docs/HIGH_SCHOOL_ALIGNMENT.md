# High School Alignment

The Hall is designed to support durable U.S. high-school English / humanities expectations while remaining broader than any one state curriculum.

## Grades 9–10

The pathway emphasizes:

- strong textual evidence;
- explicit meaning and inference;
- theme / central idea;
- characterization and development;
- structure;
- word choice, tone, and figurative language;
- source material and adaptation;
- text complexity;
- analytical and explanatory writing;
- short and sustained research;
- source evaluation;
- evidence from literature and literary nonfiction;
- collaborative discussion and presentation.

Reference:
- https://www.thecorestandards.org/ELA-Literacy/RL/9-10/
- https://www.thecorestandards.org/ELA-Literacy/W/9-10/

## Grades 11–12

The pathway adds:

- multiple interacting themes;
- ambiguity and uncertainty;
- sophisticated structure;
- satire, irony, and indirect meaning;
- multiple interpretations and adaptations;
- foundational works and literary-historical relationships;
- independent reading at the high end of the college/career-readiness band;
- argument with counterclaims;
- extended research;
- synthesis and citation;
- formal style and purposeful revision;
- evidence-based discussion.

Reference:
- https://www.thecorestandards.org/ELA-Literacy/RL/11-12/
- https://www.thecorestandards.org/ELA-Literacy/W/11-12/
- https://www.thecorestandards.org/ELA-Literacy/SL/11-12/
- https://www.thecorestandards.org/ELA-Literacy/L/11-12/

## Important Boundary

Common Core is used here as a nationally recognizable reference framework.

Formal graduation and course-credit requirements remain controlled by the learner’s jurisdiction, school, umbrella program, or other educational arrangement.
