Safety Chamber · Verified 2026-08-10

Safety comes before creativity.

The Hall uses current federal reference gateways while reminding learners that state/local adoption and requirements can differ.

Core Pattern · Clean · Separate · Cook · Chill

  • Wash hands and maintain clean tools/surfaces.
  • Separate raw animal foods and their juices from ready-to-eat foods.
  • Use a food thermometer and current official cooking guidance.
  • Refrigerate/cool promptly using appropriate methods.
  • Label allergens, dates, storage and discard/reheat plans.

Major U.S. Food Allergens

Milk, egg, fish, Crustacean shellfish, tree nuts, wheat, peanuts, soybeans and sesame. Always identify ingredients and cross-contact risk; take allergic reactions seriously.
