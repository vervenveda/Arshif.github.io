ARSHIF · Focused Reading Shelf

Reading Room

A quiet catalog of remembered scrolls — some revered, some disputed, all treated with care. This shelf is for study, comparison, and reflection, not for erasing anyone’s canon or flattening distinct traditions into one.

Archive boundary: ARSHIF preserves and labels sources. Inclusion on this shelf does not declare every work canonical, historically equivalent, or endorsed by every religious community. Follow the source and tradition-specific context attached to each work.
9 works visible
Second Temple · Ethiopic

Complete Book of Enoch

Dr. Jay Winter · Dream-visions, watchers, judgment, and cosmic order.

Preserved mainly in Ethiopic tradition; echoes in later Jewish and Christian thought about angels, the flood, and the Day of the Lord.

Second Temple · Hebrew fragments

Book of Jubilees

Ethiopic text translated by R. H. Charles · Retelling of Genesis and Exodus.

Reframes early biblical history with angelic narration, covenant themes, and a sacred calendar that shaped community rhythms.

Open source ↗
Deuterocanonical · Narrative

Tobit

Exile, healing, and angelic companionship.

A family story of loyalty and trust in God, woven with prayer, almsgiving, and the mysterious guidance of Raphael.

Open source ↗
Deuterocanonical · Wisdom

Wisdom of Ben Sira (Sirach)

Ancestral wisdom, everyday holiness, and practical ethics.

A long stream of proverbs and reflections that honor Torah, family, community, and the fear of the Lord as the beginning of wisdom.

Open source ↗
Deuterocanonical · Hellenistic

Wisdom of Solomon

Justice, immortality, and divine wisdom.

Written in a world of empires and philosophy, this work defends righteousness, meditates on the fate of the just and unjust, and portrays Wisdom as a radiant companion of God.

Open source ↗
Jewish–Christian Borderlands

1–2 Esdras

Temple rebuilding, visions, and questions about justice.

Preserves alternative tellings of return-from-exile stories and probing dialogues about suffering, empire, and divine timing.

Open study resource ↗
Early Christian · Sayings

Gospel of Thomas

A collection of sayings attributed to Jesus.

Not a narrative gospel, but a string of parables and sayings preserved in Coptic and Greek fragments, much debated and much studied.

Open source ↗
Early Christian · Visions

The Shepherd of Hermas

Visions, parables, and calls to repentance.

Once beloved in some early churches, this work imagines an angelic shepherd guiding a struggling believer through warnings and hope.

Open source ↗
Core Canon · Qur’an

The Holy Qur’an

Central scripture of Islam — recitation, guidance, and mercy.

A living canon and a touchstone for language, devotion, and careful comparison when exploring other texts and traditions in the Reading Room.
