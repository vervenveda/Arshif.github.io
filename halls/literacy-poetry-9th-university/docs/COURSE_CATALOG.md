# Poetry 101–603 Course Catalog

## Poetry 101 · Sound, Image & Meaning

**Level:** Middle School · Grade 6

**Resource modules:** Entering the Hall; Lyric Voice; Meter and Rhythm; Rhyme, Refrain, and Sound; Image and Metaphor; Children's Poetry and Nonsense

**Outcomes**
- Hear and mark sound patterns
- Explain imagery and figurative language with evidence
- Connect stanza/line choices to meaning
- Read aloud with purposeful phrasing

**Evidence:** Annotations · oral reading · craft exercises · one revised poem

**Alignment:** Grade 6: evidence, figurative language, stanza structure, speaker, performance/media comparison.

## Poetry 102 · Form, Voice & Performance

**Level:** Middle School · Grade 7

**Resource modules:** Lament and Elegy; Praise Poetry; Nature and Place; Love Poetry Across Cultures; Poetry and Music; Performance and Spoken Word

**Outcomes**
- Analyze rhyme and repeated sound
- Explain how form shapes meaning
- Compare page and performance
- Use cultural context responsibly

**Evidence:** Sound map · form analysis · recital · comparative response

**Alignment:** Grade 7: rhyme/repetition, poetic form, point of view, written/performed comparison.

## Poetry 103 · Structure, Comparison & Experiment

**Level:** Middle School · Grade 8

**Resource modules:** The Page as Field; Form Studio I: Sonnet; Form Studio III: Haiku, Tanka, Haibun; Form Studio V: Prose Poem and Free Verse; Digital, Visual, and Found Poetry; Workshop Practice

**Outcomes**
- Use strongest evidence
- Compare structures and effects
- Interpret allusion/connotation
- Give craft-based workshop feedback

**Evidence:** Comparative analysis · visual/form portfolio · workshop response

**Alignment:** Grade 8: strongest evidence, allusion, word choice, structural comparison, revision/research.

## Poetry 201 · World Poetry Reading & Traditions

**Level:** High School · Grades 9–10

**Resource modules:** Poetry Before Writing; Ancient Hymns and Sacred Songs; Epic Foundations; Wisdom Poetry; Poetry and Philosophy; Court, City, and Public Life

**Outcomes**

**Evidence:** Close-reading notebook · comparison · seminar · analysis essay

**Alignment:** Grades 9–10 literary analysis, world literature, evidence, argument, revision and research.

## Poetry 202 · Poetic Craft & Composition

**Level:** High School · Grades 9–10

**Resource modules:** Meter and Rhythm; Rhyme, Refrain, and Sound; Image and Metaphor; Form Studio I: Sonnet; Form Studio IV: Villanelle and Sestina; Form Studio V: Prose Poem and Free Verse

**Outcomes**
- Analyze form as meaning
- Use sound/rhythm intentionally
- Draft across constraints
- Revise in PROSE for precision and effect

**Evidence:** Prosody exercises · six-poem draft set · three revisions · PROSE editing pass

**Alignment:** Grades 9–10 craft, analysis, composition and substantial revision.

## Poetry 203 · Culture, Translation & Witness

**Level:** High School · Grades 11–12

**Alignment:** Grades 11–12 ambiguity, multiple interpretations, research, synthesis and formal argument.
