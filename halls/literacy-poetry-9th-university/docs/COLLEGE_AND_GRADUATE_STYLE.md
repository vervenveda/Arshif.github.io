# College & Graduate-Style Progression

There is no single national university poetry curriculum. Poetry 401–603 is therefore skills-based.

## 401–403
Close reading, prosody/form, comparative poetics, research questions, scholarly source evaluation, literary criticism, annotated bibliography, citation, seminar discussion, and research writing.

## 501–503
Theory/method, translation/reception, advanced comparative work, scholarly conversation, seminar leadership, advanced workshop, editing, sequencing, rights, publication/presentation.

## 601–603
Seminar problem-framing, historiography of scholarship, advanced bibliography, archival/digital methods, metadata/source audit, substantial research, thesis/manuscript/digital/hybrid capstone, and oral defense.

MLA undergraduate literature-research guidance informs the research ladder. The 600-series is an internal Khaemenes designation, not an institutional graduate credential.
