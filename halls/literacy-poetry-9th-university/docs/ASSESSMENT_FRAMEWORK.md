# Assessment Framework

Poetry is assessed through evidence, not one global score.

Eleven competencies:
1. Close Reading & Annotation
2. Sound, Rhythm & Prosody
3. Image, Figurative Language & Symbol
4. Form, Line & Structure
5. Speaker, Voice & Rhetoric
6. History, Culture & Tradition
7. World Poetry & Translation
8. Creative Practice & Revision
9. Workshop, Performance & Discussion
10. Research, Criticism & Citation
11. Portfolio, Publication & Capstone

Each course stores local observations as Not observed / Developing / Secure.

Suggested formal-course evidence includes annotation, analysis, performance, workshop, drafts/revision, comparison, research, bibliography, portfolio, and capstone work.
