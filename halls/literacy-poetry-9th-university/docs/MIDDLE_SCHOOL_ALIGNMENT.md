# Middle School Alignment

## Poetry 101 · Grade 6
Evidence, figurative/connotative language, stanza structure, speaker, and comparison of reading with audio/video/live performance.

## Poetry 102 · Grade 7
Rhyme and other repeated sounds, poetic form, point of view, written/performed comparison, evidence and interpretation.

## Poetry 103 · Grade 8
Strongest textual evidence, word choice, allusion, structural comparison, independent reading, argument, revision, research, credibility, and citation.

These use Common Core ELA as a widely recognizable benchmark, not as a claim that every state or learner is governed by one identical curriculum.
