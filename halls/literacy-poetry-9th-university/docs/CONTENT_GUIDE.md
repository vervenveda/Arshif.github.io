# Content Guide

New poetry dossiers should include culture/tradition, form/kind, era, tags, concise context, craft lens, and lawful reading/discovery links.

For sacred, ceremonial, restricted, Indigenous, or living traditions, use respectful and community-aware framing rather than turning restricted practice into a generic imitation prompt.

Modern copyrighted poems should generally be linked/discussed rather than reproduced in full.

New formal courses should define level, assigned modules, observable outcomes, evidence of learning, and honest alignment boundaries.
