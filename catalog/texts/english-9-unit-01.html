Unit 1 required reading · The First Annotation

Original Khaemenes Academy passage for Grade 9; available offline.

Sela read the notice once and thought she understood it. On a second reading she circled the words 'some routes' and asked whether the notice referred to every street. The difference between 'some' and 'all' changed the claim she was willing to make.

Her notebook separated the printed words from her interpretation. She could quote the notice, explain a possibility, and identify what the passage did not say.

Focus: Close reading. Read twice. Underline two specific details, explain how they support or complicate an interpretation, and write an evidence-based paragraph relevant to this unit's focus. Keep the paragraph in your existing portfolio; revisit it during Weeks 1–3.
