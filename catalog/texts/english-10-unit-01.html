The Margin Note

Original Khaemenes Academy instructional microtext. Accessible on this page without external links.

Lena underlined a claim in her first draft: The library serves everyone equally. After watching the entrance for an afternoon, she revised it: The library offers several kinds of help, but this visit cannot show whether everyone can use them. She did not erase her first sentence; she marked it as a question worth investigating.

Reading assignment for Weeks 1–3

Distinguish an observation from an inference and revise one unsupported claim.

  1. Read the passage twice and annotate two important details.
  2. Complete the unit-specific task using a short quotation and an explanation.
  3. Return to this passage in the next weekly lesson and revise your interpretation in light of feedback.
