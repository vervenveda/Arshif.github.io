# Khaemenes Field Lab

A single manifest-driven vanilla-JavaScript experiential learning engine.

## First Hall Pack
**The Living Kitchen Lab — Culinary Hall**

The learner works under realistic constraints and builds evidence rather than collecting coins.

## Core evidence
- Safety Sheet
- Recipe Card
- Cost Sheet
- Mise-en-place Plan
- Cultural Source Record
- Sensory Notes
- Reflection
- Revised Solution

## Hard safety gate
Live Service cannot open until all applicable structured safety rules pass.

The bundled Culinary Hall Pack uses an educational U.S. baseline:
- poultry: 165°F
- ground beef/pork/lamb/veal: 160°F
- whole cuts beef/pork/veal/lamb: 145°F plus 3-minute rest
- fish/shellfish: 145°F
- leftovers/casseroles: 165°F
- hot holding: 135°F or above
- cold holding: 41°F or below

A declared allergy also requires an allergen/cross-contact plan.

These are educational baseline values; real kitchens must follow current local rules, facility procedures, instructor direction, and product-specific requirements.

## Hall Pack schema
`khaemenes-field-lab-pack-v1`

Each Hall Pack can define:
- scenario pools
- competencies
- safety/ethics gates
- source doors
- unexpected events
- evidence artifacts
- theme

Future Hall Packs can include Philosophy, Theatre, Travelogues, Bible & History, Palimpsest, Poetry, Oral Traditions, World Classics, and Reading & Literacy without rewriting the engine.
