How to Use the Lab

  1. Add course context and source information, then paste or open a plain-text reading.
  2. Select exact words or sentences in the reading pane. Choose a category, write a margin note, and press Add Annotation.
  3. Use Observation for what the text demonstrably contains and Inference for what the evidence reasonably suggests.
  4. Ask Eiren for a mode-specific thinking prompt when you need a productive next question.
  5. Build a claim from your annotations in the CER workspace. Add a complication or counterreading before finalizing your interpretation.
  6. Save locally during the session. Export a JSON backup if the work matters; browser storage can be cleared by the device or user.
  7. Use Export Notes to move your analytical thinking into PROSE, a seminar, or a portfolio.
